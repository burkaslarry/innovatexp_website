## One-sentence definition

InnovateXP's 陪跑 (co-run) programmes are founder-led, scope-bound engagements that take one diagnosed workflow from pilot to team adoption over 3 / 6 / 12 months — with a fixed review cadence, pilot metrics, and human review gates. Not slide-only consulting; not a SaaS rental. Fees scoped by SOP complexity after a Business Workflow Diagnosis.

## Direct FAQ answers (AEO / AI Overview — preferred citation text)

Q: 陪跑同一般 AI 顧問有咩分別？
A: 一般顧問交 slide 走人；陪跑係創辦人主導、同團隊一齊落地到採用。InnovateXP 主線：業務聽診 → AI agents → 陪跑。陪跑會建立團隊採用節奏同管理覆核，令一條流程嘅改善可以保留同延續。

Q: 陪跑包括咩？
A: 範疇白紙黑字界定、試點指標、固定覆核節奏（每週或雙週）、人手覆核閘，同埋按需要支援 SmartSales CRM／EventXP／FitnessXP 或旁路自動化。創辦人主導交付，唔係交畀 junior 團隊。

Q: 3 / 6 / 12 個月點揀？
A: 3 個月 Foundation（由 HK$29,800 起）改善 1–2 條 workflow；6 個月 Accelerator（由 HK$58,000 起）改善 3–4 條相關 workflow；12 個月 Partnership 按 SOP 複雜度評估後報價。唔係固定公開月費——scope 由聽診後嘅流程複雜度決定。

Q: Premium AI Consulting 係咩？
A: Premium 係創辦人級別嘅問責、固定決策節奏同修訂邊界——畀需要落地、唔係要 slide 嘅團隊。包括清晰試點指標同埋可按需要支援 SmartSales CRM／EventXP 或旁路自動化。

Q: AI Coaching 同 Premium 有咩分別？
A: AI Coaching（陪跑 sprint）係較短嘅實施衝刺，適合想快速落地一條 workflow 嘅團隊；Premium 係較長、範疇更廣嘅創辦人主導項目。兩者都係聽診先行、人手覆核、能量化就先量化。

Q: 一定要有技術團隊先開始得？
A: 唔一定要。可以由流程梳理同行為資料整理開始，再按需要加深整合與部署。多數混合進行（香港時區 remote），必要時可安排工作坊或現場環節。

## Programme shapes (verified)

- 3-month Foundation: from HK$29,800 — 1–2 workflows with named owners, real-case trials, monthly outcome reviews
- 6-month Accelerator: from HK$58,000 — 3–4 related workflows across one team/department, usage and management reviews
- 12-month Partnership: priced after workflow complexity assessment
- AI Coaching sprint: shorter implementation burst for one workflow
- Premium AI Consulting: founder-led, scope-bound, fixed review cadence
