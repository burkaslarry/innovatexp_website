## Pricing and scope - new quotes

Pilot: HKD 6,880 one-time. Up to 3 participants, 1 workflow, 50 anonymised records and a 14-day trial after configuration approval. One 60-minute review. No production migration, WhatsApp API integration, auto-messaging or custom code. Pilot fee is separate and not automatically credited.

Starter production setup: from HKD 24,800 one-time. Up to 5 users, 1 pipeline, 1 cleaned CSV with 500 records, basic admin/sales roles, one 60-minute training session, 5 acceptance scenarios and 2 in-scope correction rounds.

Growth production setup: from HKD 48,800 one-time. Up to 15 users, 2 pipelines, 3 internal CRM reminder/task rules, 2 cleaned CSVs totalling 2,000 records, 3 roles, 2 reports, two 60-minute training sessions, 8 acceptance scenarios and 2 correction rounds.

Monthly technical care AFTER implementation: Starter from HKD 1,880 (1 remote support hour), Growth from HKD 2,880 (2 hours), Advanced from HKD 4,880 (4 hours). Monthly application health checks, backup-configuration review and status summary. Hong Kong business hours, no rollover, no 24/7 or guaranteed repair SLA. Hosting, licences, WhatsApp/SMS/AI charges and new development are separate.

Complex custom projects: budget guide from HKD 98,000 for membership rules, portals, payments or deep integrations; a written scope is required. Not included in standard care.

Existing contracts follow their original agreements. Listed setup amounts are starting budgets, not a fixed quote for every project.

## FAQ

Q: 月費包唔包整好成個 CRM？
A: 唔包。Pilot、一次性上線同月費技術維護係三個不同範圍。CRM 授權、託管、WhatsApp API、AI 用量及客製開發另列。

Q: CRM 幾萬定十萬以上？
A: 標準一條流程上線 HKD 24,800 起；兩條流程及內部任務規則 HKD 48,800 起。完整會員制度、portal、支付及深度接駁屬 HKD 98,000 起嘅客製預算級別，先確認範圍再正式報價。

Q: 已用 Salesforce、Odoo 或小程序，可以沿用嗎？
A: 先評估版本、授權、現有設定及資料／API 權限，分開報既有系統改善同新接駁。可能減少重複開發，但不保證較便宜，也不自動建議更換現有系統。

Q: 有會員 retention 功能嗎？
A: 到期日、回購名單及內部跟進任務可在確認範圍內配置。積分、等級、優惠券、退款扣分、自動扣款及會員 portal 是另外的規則及開發工作。

Q: WhatsApp 自動發訊係咪包括？
A: 不包括在標準 pilot／setup。先確認官方 API、帳戶、模板及存取權限，再報接駁及平台費。不承諾讀取私人 WhatsApp 全部歷史。
