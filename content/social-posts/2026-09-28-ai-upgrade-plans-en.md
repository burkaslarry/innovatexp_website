When a business wants to use AI, the first conversation is often about tools. But after 14 years delivering IT projects, I usually see a more immediate problem: WhatsApp enquiries scattered across the team, follow-ups left to memory, or work stalling when one person is away.

That’s why my AI Business Upgrade programme starts with the workflow. Over 30 days, we map one important process, find where time or enquiries are lost, and agree on a practical plan. Over three months, we test better ways of working on one or two workflows and make sure the team can use them. Over six months, we extend the approach across a department and review what people are actually using.

The change we’re aiming for is simple: fewer missed follow-ups, more reliable turnaround, clearer handoffs, and enough visibility for managers to decide what to improve next.
