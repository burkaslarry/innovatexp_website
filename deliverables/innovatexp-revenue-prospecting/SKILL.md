# InnovateXP revenue prospecting

Help Larry Lo turn evidenced operational needs into useful conversations, questionnaire responses, qualified opportunities and paid work. Use the relevant sales workflow; do not instantiate all 50 prompt personas or replace the user's channel with Raised Hand or another platform.

## Target and offer

- Target SMEs with **3–60 people**, retaining Hong Kong and expanding to **Southeast Asia and Europe**. Record actual country, operating team and timezone; headquarters alone does not prove operational presence in a target market.
- Prioritize training/education and professional services. Fitness, clinics, events and other service SMEs can qualify when their repeated workflow fits an actual InnovateXP offer. Prefer owners and operational decision makers to unrelated employees.
- Diagnose one workflow before choosing tools: scattered enquiries, slow quotes, bookings/payments, course operations, event follow-up or handovers dependent on one person. Match consulting, SmartSales, EventXP, AccountXP, FitnessXP or Online Presence to evidenced needs.
- Current offer/pricing sources take precedence over old posters, PDFs and examples. In the website repository use `src/content/pricing.ts` and the relevant service page. Do not sell a ≤10-person package to a 60-person team at the same price; confirm larger-team scope.
- Use https://innovatexp.co/bookme as the questionnaire and 30-minute diagnosis entry. Verify language and availability before promising a booking, local-language delivery or local-currency price. English is a practical default when language is unknown; do not fabricate fluency.

## Qualification from evidence

Read [questionnaire-qualification.md](references/questionnaire-qualification.md) when qualifying prospects or preparing calls. Use the real questions as the research framework; public evidence may answer only some. Never complete or submit a prospect's form using guessed answers.

Record source URL, publication date (or unknown), checked date, country, size evidence, role, problem evidence and unanswered questions. Distinguish:

1. **Explicit request:** asks for help/provider recommendations for a matching workflow.
2. **Problem expressed:** describes a concrete problem without requesting a provider.
3. **Indirect signal:** hiring, growth, events, tool changes. Suggests research, not confirmed need.
4. **Profile-only fit:** relevant industry/title without problem evidence. Exploratory, not qualified.

Prioritize unresolved requests and prospect-supplied questionnaire/reply evidence. Default research window is 30 days; check older signals for continuing relevance. Hiring/funding alone does not establish demand for external services, budget or authority.

Mark size **confirmed**, **overlapping band** or **unknown**. A 2–10 or 51–200 band does not prove 3–60 eligibility. A 31+ questionnaire answer needs a 31–60 versus 61+ clarification. Do not fill requested counts with invented or weakly matched accounts.

A **qualified lead** has supported geography/size fit, a concrete matching problem, a responsible contact and willingness to explore a next step. Keep budget, timing and buying authority as separate known/unknown fields. Quick appointment timing is not proof of purchase intent. Recommend contact, research first, nurture or skip with reasons. Profile-only outreach may be prepared or sent within the user's scope, but stays exploratory and never counts as qualified.

For acquisition options read [lead-generation.md](references/lead-generation.md). Research/drafts do not authorize posts, messages, ads, partner communications or future scheduled sending. Reuse explicit authorization for the same campaign; do not ask again for every routine message. Revising this skill does not authorize a campaign.

## Personalization and handoff

Use an evidence-based opening, a relevant offer connection, one focused question and one modest next step. Avoid unsupported ROI, invented pain and generic flattery. Prepare any promised checklist or outline before offering it.

When evidence is incomplete, ask about one relevant questionnaire field instead of asserting a problem. After a useful reply, offer the booking questionnaire for deeper qualification. Do not force a booking link into every first message. Preserve the prospect's terminology and label hypotheses.

## Sending and recovery

Read [tracking.md](references/tracking.md) before sending/reporting. Load existing records and conversation history; deduplicate canonical recipient URL and company/domain. Prefer one active contact per company unless the user requests a buying-committee approach. Respect opt-outs and active conversations.

Before each send, independently verify the visible recipient name, company/domain and **actual final composer text** against that recipient's draft. Re-check after edits/navigation. Stop on a mismatch; do not trust reused variables or assume a text replacement succeeded. Record sent only when the message appears in the correct conversation or the platform confirms success. A clicked button is not sending evidence.

For an ambiguous result, inspect the conversation before retrying. For an incorrect sent message, acknowledge the error, use supported editing when available, verify correction and record the incident without counting another recipient. Do not delete without the authorization required by current policies.

Stop the affected sending flow on a platform limit, CAPTCHA, restriction or unresolved delivery failure. Save drafts and the blocker; do not evade limits through alternate accounts, bulk tooling or repeated attempts. Report the affected channel, confirmed count, remaining target and known reset time without guessing. Continue independent research where useful. Do not imply background continuation or schedule future sending unless requested and authorized.

## Results

Report researched accounts, exploratory outreach, verified sends, replies, positive replies, qualified leads, confirmed/attended meetings, proposals, won revenue and collected payments separately. Specify currency, cohort and time window. Unchecked replies are unknown, not zero. Contacting 100 means 100 unique verified recipients, not clicks, drafts or searches. Quality, authorization and platform limits take precedence over filling a count.
