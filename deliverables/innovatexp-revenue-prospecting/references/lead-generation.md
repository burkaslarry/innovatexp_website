# Acquisition options

Choose by evidenced need, reachable buyer and regional delivery fit. Options are not standing permission to publish, contact or spend.

| Channel | Approach | Evidence / next step |
|---|---|---|
| Public buying requests | LinkedIn posts and relevant local communities: quote workflows, CRM selection, bookings/payment reconciliation, course operations, event follow-up. Read requests and replies. | Dated unresolved request, location, size, role and offer fit. Respond to that request. |
| Existing relationships | Review relevant connections and conversations; a warm relationship differs from an unanswered broadcast. | Latest interaction, earlier offers, opt-outs. Relevant re-engagement question. |
| Questionnaire inbound | Review user-supplied or authorized accessible booking/questionnaire records. | Real answers and unknowns; form submitted versus meeting confirmed versus paid. |
| Partner referrals | Education providers, SME consultants, chambers/business networks. | Narrow referral brief: 3–60, served countries, one pain and booking entry. Permissioned introduction; no private list sharing. |
| Useful content/workshops | Demonstrate enquiry-to-quote ownership or course enrolment/payment reconciliation. | Prepare a usable checklist/example; diagnostic question then questionnaire. Downloads/attendance alone are weak intent. |
| Regional directories | Education providers, consultancies and service SMEs in selected SEA/European countries. Verify operating presence. | Directories establish account fit, not buying intent. Research current needs. |
| Event follow-up | Authorized attendee enquiries/questions/requested follow-ups. | Document reason per person; attendee list is not blanket outreach permission. |
| Website conversion | Relevant service content/cases to questionnaire/diagnosis or approved fixed-scope product. | Track consent-appropriate source tags and real submissions/bookings; website changes need a separate implementation task. |

For each campaign propose country/niche, problem being tested, channel, evidence requirement, resource, next step and success measure. Start with an evidence-rich cohort and adjust from replies/qualified meetings. More messages and wider geography do not guarantee sales.

Questionnaire answers and public requests outrank inferred hiring/funding signals. Verify applicable regional channel/platform requirements through current official guidance when needed. Do not purchase lists, ads or enrichment subscriptions without authorization.

Example only with verified need: “Saw your request for a way to reconcile course bookings and payments. We diagnose those workflows before choosing tools. Is the main gap payment matching or ownership of follow-up?”

Without a stated problem, mention the verified service and ask about its workflow. Log exploratory; do not assert lost leads or assume budget.
